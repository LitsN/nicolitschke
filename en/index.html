About Services Experience Texts

Hello!

I am Nico Litschke, and I am responsible for the safety of software-intensive systems.

From team lead to CTO, I hear the same questions in R&D again and again:

  • Which design delivers the system's functions, features and safety at the same time?
  • What can I do so that my developers and experts take ownership of their share of safety?
  • How do I put our limited resources where they matter?
  • How do I minimise technical, economic, environmental and liability risks?
  • How do I back this up with safety evidence, and which safety standards apply?
  • And lately: how can I use AI in system functions and in safety development?

Answering these questions is my daily work. As a System Safety Engineer I have written and owned functional and technical safety concepts from QM to ASIL D; those systems are on the road in millions of vehicles today. Among them electric drives, brakes and sensors replaced by AI models. Most recently the same approach proved itself in industrial robotics, on safe object detection up to PL e.

Services

Lifecycle

From the applicable standards I determine the relevant parts and assemble them into your individual safety lifecycle – including the question of which methods and test scopes each part of the system requires.

ResultA tailored safety lifecycle based on the relevant standards. A safety work breakdown structure and a safety plan that you only need to fold into your project planning.
