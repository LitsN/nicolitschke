Approach

What makes a design safe?

Standards such as ISO 26262 describe how to verify a given system. That matters, and it feeds into the safety evidence. What makes a system design safe, they do not answer.

I close this gap with STPA. The analysis looks at the whole system of humans and machines and shows where inadequate control leads to hazards. Robustness analyses such as FMEA or FTA hardly capture that: they examine individual components.
